# Privacy Policy

## Tontreffer

**Effective Date: 2026-11-05**

## 1. Summary

Tontreffer does not collect, store or share any personal data.
It works entirely offline, has no account and contains no analytics,
advertising or tracking.

## 2. Microphone

Tontreffer needs access to the microphone to hear you sing and measure your
pitch.
The audio is analysed on your device while an exercise is running and is
discarded right away.
It is never recorded to a file, stored or sent anywhere.
You can revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

Tontreffer stores your settings on your device, such as your voice range,
concert pitch, accuracy level and chosen sound.
They never leave your device, except as part of a device backup that you
have set up with your operating system.
Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## 4. No Network Access

Tontreffer makes no network connections.
On Android, it does not even request permission to access the internet.

## 5. App Stores

If you install Tontreffer from an app store, such as Google Play or the App
Store, the store operator may process data about you under its own privacy
policy.
We receive only anonymous statistics from the store, such as the number of
installations and crash reports.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We will post any changes to this Privacy Policy on this page.
The effective date at the top indicates when it was last revised.
